## Approved conventions

1. **Town Phase:** Each party normally receives **4 TAP**. Preserve the Journey results that expressly change the allowance, including their existing conditions and costs. A reference to **3 TAP** in such an exception is not a different default.
2. **Druidic tradition:** Use **Druidic Rituals**. Preserve the five distinct traditions and their existing minor-ritual terminology, casting attributes, costs, and effects.
3. **Reference hierarchy:** The reference aid is **Appendix C. Rules Reference**. If a summary conflicts with a full rule, use the full rule in the cited main section. Do not call this reference aid **Section 12** or renumber the manuscript to match an older brief.
4. **Armour and reactions:** Under **5.4.3**, whenever a combatant would suffer **blunt**, **piercing**, or **slashing** damage, resolve its applicable armour mitigation regardless of the damage source. Roll equipped Chest **AD**, if any, and add every applicable source of flat **AV**. This is automatic, costs no **AP**, is not a **Reaction**, and applies when Reactions are unavailable. A combatant with listed flat **AV**, such as a **Monster**, **Hireling**, or most **Summons**, applies that value instead of rolling Chest AD. Shield **AD** and **AV** apply only through **Block**. Damage of other types and HP loss ignore AV. Preserve specific exceptions and rules that reduce, halve, or ignore the total AV. **Brace** is not a current reaction; do not restore it from an older brief.
5. **Monster Checks:** Under **2.3**, a **Monster** never makes an ability **Check** and has no **STR**, **DEX**, **INT**, or **SPI** score. If a rule would require such a Check, apply its failed-Check effect automatically unless the Monster is **Immune**. If a **Power** or scroll requires such a Monster Check and the Monster has the **Elite** Role or a **LVL** that exceeds the caster's Level, make the **Casting Check** with **Disadvantage** (Elite clause added in RC519 by owner-delegated decision, 2026-10-27). Do not substitute `1d20 + ML`. Preserve distinct Monster damage rolls, Morale rolls, and other separately specified dice procedures.
