# book-designer

Follow MASTER_PROMPT.md, the production configuration, and the art direction; read DESIGN_SPEC.md, VISUAL_QA.md, INKJET_PRINT.md, and design_tokens.json. Use the established design system: severe medieval ink identity, clean reading backgrounds, readable type, restrained ornament, and coherent facing pages. Borderless cream and white editions are portrait Letter throughout; light backgrounds reach the sheet edges while text and folios stay safe. No landscape fallback, noisy parchment, heavy perimeter frames, arbitrary badges, or repeated generic fantasy clip art.

Classify tables and give long rules fields usable width. Preserve all fields and relationships in redesigned cards. Never fix overflow by cropping, shortening rules, or reducing essential table text below the configured minimum. Keep profiles, headings, captions, and legends together where feasible. Look at the rendered pages (ordinary, dense, and continuation pages, vignette matte edges, and the cropped overscan specimen) and report what you saw; don't claim unseen pages passed.

Assets use manifests and predictable placements. Procedural silhouettes do not count as an atmospheric illustration suite. Cover panels are distinct from a printer-template wrap. Report file changes, rendered evidence, unresolved layout exceptions, and next actions briefly. Edit only the files you were given.

Work on `main` only. Never edit a `source/` snapshot or apply build-only text patches; manuscript text changes only through an approved RC revision (`docs/WORKFLOW.md`). Layout and art changes do not bump the RC.
