# Heraldry PDF pipeline, density, and layout audit

Research date: 2026-10-16. Read-only implementation review, all-page geometry census, and bounded visual inspection. No manuscript, template, token, release ledger, or production PDF was changed or rebuilt. Recommendations below are proposals for a controlled layout iteration.

## What should the next iteration trial and verify?

### Takeaway

Keep11pt rules type, existing margins, and the heraldic identity for the first density trial. Recover space through a unified style contract, measured composition, smaller table insets, moderate paragraph/list spacing, and explicit continuation design; only then assess whether a10.5pt alternative is worth reopening the existing11pt lock.

- **Mandatory rule interpretation:** a complete source section/scenario/table/procedure/entry that fits an empty usable page at its approved width stays on that physical page, including heading, notes, figure, and caption. Move it whole when necessary. A truly oversized parent may continue with identifying furniture and intact fit-capable children. Making every arbitrarily long section unbreakable would overflow; shrinking content, omitting notes, and enlarging a group artificially to evade the keep rule are expressly prohibited. The owner clarification already contains this conditional policy; do not weaken it to fill pages. [Exact clarification](C:/Heraldry/art-producer/docs/DESIGN_REFINEMENT.md:86).
- **Reduce whitespace without breaking the rule:** optimise only within the same section; rebalance permitted pieces, relocate that section's own approved art, reduce excessive panel/table overhead, measure full-width alternatives where approved, and pull a short final child onto the preceding page if the complete measured composition fits. Preserve forced starts at major sections/scenarios, intact children, and intentional whitespace that remains. Never fill a gap with unrelated next-section prose. [Policy](C:/Heraldry/art-producer/docs/DESIGN_REFINEMENT.md:55), [same-section fill order](C:/Heraldry/art-producer/docs/BOOK_DESIGN_PLAN.md:457).
- **Refine the aesthetic grammar:** retain blackletter at major boundaries, serif prose, sans data, clear black header bands, and small heraldic navigation. Give table continuations an identifying name and `continued`, make scenario running heads scenario-specific, align numeric columns by their actual semantic alignment, and keep caption treatment proportional to figure size. Prefer fewer component transitions and consistent baseline/spacing relationships to more boxes, more decoration, or larger spot art. [Existing identity](C:/Heraldry/art-producer/typst/templates/system.typ:1), [continuation contract](C:/Heraldry/art-producer/docs/DESIGN_REFINEMENT.md:30).

The balancer freezes a segment after more than2flips and exits at its pass limit without a separate convergence failure. Log per-stage time/RSS, input hashes, pass count, final balance signature and convergence; cache measurement/layout only by complete relevant source/font/style/asset hashes, and keep every release gate. Compiler reuse within an isolated process is already implemented, and QA shares one query cache within verify.py; preserve those wins.
