# Make Heraldry's layout denser without crowding

Review date: 2026-10-16. **Review and proposals only; no layout implementation or PDF build.**

Heraldry should recover space through more accurate composition, smaller component overhead, and better continuation handling before reducing its rules type. The strongest verified defect is in the evidence pathway: a keep/table page map names different physical pages from the exact PDF whose hash it records. Several implementation inconsistencies also undermine predictable layout, including competing style definitions, mismatched table measurements, discarded right alignment, and missing continuation identity. The existing 11 pt serif text, quiet reading field, heraldic navigation, and subject-specific ink art already form a useful visual foundation. Keep that foundation, make ordinary rules consistently two columns, and give opening flavour and genuinely wide tables or diagrams explicit spanning roles. Wider margins would reduce usable space; a modest spacing and table-inset trial is a better first density experiment. **No defensible page-saving percentage exists yet**, because the latest RC493 manuscript has not been compiled under either a baseline or a proposed layout in this review.

## Repair measurement and continuation defects before shrinking type

### The baseline is an exact released PDF, not a current-source build

The inspected cream interior is **RC492-build-37: 672 pages, 148,711,078 bytes, SHA-256 `10e4f8bea8cff69404a6ee1385077e0533b767ff1815714cd0b2532e245a8e78`**. Its bytes were downloaded and independently checked against the [release manifest](../dist/RELEASE.json). A different same-named PDF in the old checkout was excluded. The active source is RC493, and current figures and code include later work; this report therefore distinguishes released-page observations from live-code findings. No RC493 PDF, new release approval, or physical proof is claimed. All page numbers below are **one-based physical PDF viewer pages**, not printed folios. Full evidence and method are in the [pipeline research note](../research_notes/Rulebook%20layout%20and%20density%20review/pipeline_audit.md).

An automated geometry census covered all 672 pages. Direct visual review covered **25 distinct cream-interior pages**, including ordinary rules, tables, power entries, scenario material, reference pages, and sparse continuations. The census identified 32 pages with a text/image vertical union below 25% of the body height, 158 with an empty full-width horizontal band over 250 pt, and 56 with strongly asymmetric column text counts. These are triage signals, **not percentages of wasted area or confirmed defects**: vector drawings are excluded, transparent image bounds count as occupied, and wide objects complicate column classification. Fresh visual review did not cover the digital edition, white interior, separate covers, calibration, or overscan exports. Their implementation paths and earlier evidence were examined, without renewing their visual approval.

Good ordinary-page composition remains visible on pp36, 300, 400, and 450. The problems are concentrated in transitions and tails: p24 carries a short lore tail before a major-section start; pp92, 123, and 162 have short examples or notes; p187 contains the last two rows of a long potion table; p249 has power entries confined to the right column; p517 has a small scenario victory tail; p643 has two bullets; and p667 contains the closing Elevation bullet of the source's named Single-Sheet Rules Summary. These deserve controlled repair, but a sparse page alone does not establish a keep violation or permission to remove content. The intentionally blank **p6 verso remains accepted and must be preserved**. Its treatment is recorded in the [visual review](../production/qa/visual-review.json#L729) and implemented in [frontmatter.typ:106](../typst/frontmatter.typ#L106); older prose opposing a forced blank verso needs reconciliation with that settled decision.

### The highest-priority bug is a page-map discrepancy

The hash-bound keep report places `h-c5-reactions-summary` on pp640-641 and T327 on p641; the table audit also assigns T327 to p641. In the actual hash-verified PDF, independent PyMuPDF and pypdf extraction put **C.5 on p638, its reactions table on p639, and C.6/Critical Hits on p641**. The PDF bookmark also points to C.5 at p638. This is a confirmed evidence mismatch. It does **not** prove that the reactions table is split, content is missing, or every historical visual finding is wrong. Its cause is unresolved. Both the query and compile code explicitly request cream/print, so an asserted digital/interior mix-up would be speculation. The separate query compiler and its cache/input identity need a controlled reproduction. ([Keep evidence](../production/qa/keep-audit.json#L21470), [table evidence](../production/qa/table-audit.json#L9028), [query process](../tools/query_typst.py#L8), [verification process](../tools/verify.py#L44).)

Repair this before treating zero-split reports as conclusive. Positions should originate from the same final compilation as the serialized PDF, carry complete input and variant identities, and be checked against actual PDF anchor destinations or recognisable text. Independently enumerate required source units so an omitted unit cannot disappear from both the generated log and its audit. The recorded 1,557 audited/1,250 fit-capable units and zero avoidable splits remain historical results, qualified by the demonstrated mismatch. ([Audit implementation](../tools/keep_audit.py#L19).)

## Tighten spacing and table overhead while retaining 11 pt rules

### The current type is already within useful book-design ranges

Live settings are **Source Serif 4, 11 pt body; additive leading 3.55 pt; paragraph space 6.5 pt; table type 9.3 pt**. The physical frame is 7.2 x 9.62 in, with two 3.48 in columns and a 0.24 in alley. Margins are inside 0.75 in, outside 0.55 in, top 0.68 in, and bottom 0.70 in. These implemented values supersede the older declarative JSON for describing today's layout. The released PDF's prevalent measured body baseline separation is **15.10 pt**, derived from 16,496 neighbouring same-x/same-size line pairs. ([Live tokens](../typst/templates/system.typ#L24), [paragraph settings](../typst/heraldry.typ#L190).)

The distinction between baseline distance and the engine parameter matters. Typst defines `leading` as the gap between text edges; baseline distance also depends on those edges. The current 3.55 pt value is therefore not a line-height ratio or a 3.55 pt baseline. Its measured 15.10/11 ratio is about 137%, within the general 120-145% baseline guidance. RPG-specific guidance suggests body text around 10-12 pt and 45-75 characters per line; these are specimen starting points, not laws. Typeface size and apparent size differ. ([Typst paragraph reference](https://typst.app/docs/reference/model/par/), [Notestine, Typography 101](https://www.explorersdesign.com/typography-101/), [Butterick, line spacing](https://practicaltypography.com/line-spacing.html), [point size](https://practicaltypography.com/point-size.html).)

**Do not globally reduce the body font first.** Eleven-point rules are already owner-locked in the [design plan](../docs/BOOK_DESIGN_PLAN.md#L64). Preserve that setting for the first trial. If spacing and flow repairs prove insufficient, compare a separate 10.5 pt specimen only after explicitly reopening that lock. Do not compensate for broken fitting by shrinking isolated pages. Preserve the project's 9 pt essential-type floor; current 9.3 pt table text leaves little reason to shrink tables globally.

### A conservative trial changes component costs, not the reading system

| Table text/inset | 9.3 pt; horizontal 5 pt, vertical 4 pt | **9.3 pt unchanged**; horizontal 4.5 pt, vertical 3 pt | Wrapping, row height, scannability, header separation |
| Table outer spacing | 10 pt above, 12 pt below | 8 pt above, 9 pt below | Attachment of heading, caption, and notes |
| Example-panel inset | Left 11/right 9/top 8/bottom 9 pt | Left 9/right 8/top 6/bottom 7 pt | Text measure, distinct panel edge, readable breathing room |
| Margins and alley | Values above | **Unchanged** | Facing-page comfort, printer/binding safety |
| Intro flavour | Chapter lead spans; most other flavour stays in a column | Explicit opening region, roughly 80-82% of useful width, then two-column rules | Actual character measure and title/art/lead relationship |
| Balancing | Fixed reserves | Measured composition with evidenced tolerance | Fit boundaries, sparse tails, convergence |

These are **proposed engine tokens, not tested final settings**. Test spacing-only, table-only, and combined variants against the same current-source content. A 0.4 pt baseline reduction is approximately 2.65% of the observed line pitch; it is not a 2.65% book reduction. Reducing table vertical inset by 1 pt above and below saves 2 pt per unchanged-height row: a 20-row table could recover 40 pt before changes in wrapping, headers, or keeps. Smaller horizontal inset can also prevent a wrap. This is why table boxes are a more promising initial target than smaller table letters. ([Current component values](../typst/heraldry.typ#L379).)

**Wider margins will not directly lower page count.** They remove usable width and height. Adding 0.10 in to each horizontal margin reduces the current 7.2 in frame to 7.0 in, about 2.78% less horizontal capacity before reflow. Wider margins can improve binding comfort or shorten an excessive line measure, but this two-column page already separates those problems. Retain the current margins until a physical proof identifies a reason to change them. General single-column margin prescriptions should not be transplanted unchanged into this layout. ([Butterick, page margins](https://practicaltypography.com/page-margins.html).)

Comfortable compact book typography also differs from accessible clear-print design. RNIB's product-instruction guidance uses an Arial-equivalent 14 pt minimum and alternative formats; a 9-11 pt compact edition does not satisfy that recommendation merely because commercial games use small type. Consider larger-print or reflow-friendly output separately. WCAG's cited resize criterion concerns resizing to 200% without lost function/content, not a 9 pt print minimum. ([RNIB guidance, PDF pp3 and 8](https://www.rnib.org.uk/documents/2272/Complete_Product_Design_Guide.pdf), [W3C SC 1.4.4](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/resize-text.html).)

## Use one prose grammar and borrow reference solutions selectively

### Two-column rules need explicit opening and spanning roles

Adopt **two columns for ordinary substantive rules prose**, with a distinct single-column introduction region for chapter/section flavour. A single-column intro need not run prose across the entire 7.2 in width: an inset measure can retain calm reading while the title/art span the page. Current code gives the first italic chapter lead a special component, while most section/scenario flavour passes through generic column-level paragraph handling. Introduce a semantic role keyed to stable source IDs and heading rank. Do not interpret every italic paragraph as opening flavour or accidentally promote mechanical text to a broad line. ([Chapter extraction](../tools/build_book.py#L421), [generic flavour](../tools/build_book.py#L278), [lead component](../typst/templates/system.typ#L312).)

Retain deliberate full-width tables, diagrams, and reference regions. Forcing every non-flavour object into half a page would make many comparison tables taller and diagrams smaller. Spanning content should return cleanly to two-column prose without resetting or stranding a small rules tail. Typst's ordinary column container does not automatically balance heights; page-level columns offer a different architecture, but changing architecture requires a protected prototype against Heraldry's existing planner. Local `sticky` behaviour alone does not implement whole-section keeps. ([Typst columns](https://typst.app/docs/reference/layout/columns/), [blocks](https://typst.app/docs/reference/layout/block/).)

The common lesson is **information-specific width and controlled hierarchy**, rather than maximum density. A practising tabletop designer likewise stresses consistent treatments, short useful columns, and enough whitespace for both learning and reference use. RPG grid guidance starts with type/measure before deriving the page grid. ([Matt Paquette, Rulebooks 101](https://www.mattpaquette.com/design-blog/2018/7/9/tabletop-graphic-design-rulebooks), [Notestine, Grid System 101](https://www.explorersdesign.com/grid-system-101-a-beginners-glossary/).)

For Heraldry's appearance, retain blackletter at major boundaries, serif rules, sans data, restrained black table heads, and useful heraldic running labels. Standardise heading spacing, caption scale, list indents, note attachment, and continuation names. Let narrow numeric/roll columns release width to explanations; respect numeric alignment; compare fine horizontal rules/light bands with fully boxed cells where the data permits. A prose-dominant two-field table can be compared with a labelled list only if every cell, label, relationship, and source-order position survives. Native tables should retain semantic repeated headers on genuine continuations. ([Typst table guide](https://typst.app/docs/guides/tables/), [Notestine, RPG lists](https://www.explorersdesign.com/how-to-design-lists-in-rpgs/).)

Use approved art at meaningful openings and as occasional related vignettes. Art must yield before it fragments a fit-capable rules unit. More texture, borders, and boxes would make the reading field busier without repairing composition. Quiet linked contents, precise cross-reference destinations, scenario-specific running heads, and clear folios will contribute more to finish. Visual beauty does not establish tagged-PDF accessibility: native semantics and logical reading order must also survive moved or floating content. ([Hyperlink design](https://www.explorersdesign.com/how-to-hyperlink/), [Typst accessibility](https://typst.app/docs/guides/accessibility/).)

## Preserve complete units and make whitespace explainable

The owner's keep rule is already conditional and workable: **a complete section, subsection, scenario, table, procedure, profile, or entry that fits an empty usable page at its approved width stays on one physical page**, including its heading and necessary notes, figure, and caption. Move it whole when the remaining frame is too short. A genuinely oversized parent can continue, with identifying continuation furniture and intact fit-capable children. Do not enlarge a grouping to evade the rule, omit notes, clip overflow, or shrink a unit until it fits. A page-contained section can use an approved internal two-column composition; keeping it together does not require a giant single-column block. ([Owner clarification](../docs/DESIGN_REFINEMENT.md#L86).)

Separate that keep rule from a **fresh-page start**. Chapters, agreed major sections, and named scenarios can start fresh, while minor headings still receive mandatory complete-unit protection. The source-rank mapping must be explicit before implementation; conflicting documentation cannot justify silently weakening the owner's policy. Some end whitespace is an inevitable consequence of keeping the next unit whole or honouring a major start. General widow/orphan guidance also recognises this trade-off, although Heraldry's full-unit requirement is stronger. ([Existing policy](../docs/DESIGN_REFINEMENT.md#L51), [Butterick, widow/orphan control](https://practicaltypography.com/widow-and-orphan-control.html).)

Reduce **unnecessary** space within the same section: remove duplicate wrapper gaps, improve table allocations, compose the columns at their actual widths, reposition that section's own art, and bring a short final child back only when the complete measured arrangement fits. Preserve source order. Do not backfill an unrelated later rule into the hole. Classify large voids as fresh-start policy, next-unit keep, actual content end, overestimated height, unmatched column segment, or decoration displacement. The latter implementation causes deserve repair first; the first three can be correct. ([Same-section fill order](../docs/BOOK_DESIGN_PLAN.md#L457).)

A deterministic planner should explain its result. Record each kept unit's approved width, measured height, available frame, selected composition, continuation reason, and final page set. Treat final paragraph fragments, isolated result rows, and unnamed continuations as explicit review targets. For p187, the objective is both better table allocation and a named continuation if the table remains oversized. For p516-517, test a complete scenario composition and movement of its own art before accepting a tiny victory tail. The p666-667 reference must preserve every rule; its source title is not authority to delete content or rename the entry through layout.

The runtime pathway also needs better evidence before optimisation. A clean build performs a measurement query, up to eight cream balancing compiles, two more interior variants, three extra cover/calibration compiles, and a separate QA query compiler. Compiler reuse and a shared verification query cache already help. The balancer freezes a segment after more than two flips and can exit at its pass limit without a distinct convergence failure. Add stage timings, peak memory, pass counts, final balance signatures, and an explicit convergence result. Cache only against complete relevant source/font/style/asset hashes, and retain every release gate. No speedup is claimed without measurement. ([Build loop](../tools/build_book.py#L872), [compiler reuse](../tools/typst_run.py#L27), [verification cache](../tools/verify.py#L44).)

## Prove the next design on current-source specimens

First, repair the position-evidence discrepancy and establish one effective style contract. Add meaningful regressions for C.5/T327, right alignment, unique start/end markers, source-unit coverage, named continuations, and rank-specific starts. Preserve manuscript bytes and all current figure bindings. These repairs provide a trustworthy basis for density decisions; they do not require a new manuscript revision.

Next, compile a **12-20-page current-source stress set** with neighbouring content included. It should cover a good ordinary page, chapter/section flavour, the pp30/57 column cases, p91-92 example tail, p186-187 table tail, p249 power continuation, p516-517 scenario tail, p638-639 reactions transition, and p666-667 reference tail. Released page numbers identify the problems; current stable IDs must identify the actual specimens. Compare baseline, spacing-only, table-only, and combined trials before freezing settings. Check both facing-page positions and 100% viewing, then print representative cream/white pages at actual size. The first test retains 11 pt body, current margins, essential-type floor, and accepted p6 treatment.

Acceptance must be measurable. Require **zero missing/duplicate/out-of-order text blocks or cells; zero altered mechanical values; zero missing expected source IDs; zero avoidable splits of fit-capable units; zero unresolved queried-versus-serialized anchor page discrepancies; zero overlaps, clipping, unsafe essential text, orphan headings/captions, or unnamed genuine continuations**. Preserve all 57 current source-bound figures, readable labels, links/bookmarks, metadata, and source exceptions. Record baseline pitch, line-length distribution, hyphen/runt frequency, row wraps, effective figure scale, and whitespace causes. Compare cream/white glyph and figure coordinates, allowing substrate differences while requiring the same intended geometry; do not rely on matching page text alone.

Only after the specimen succeeds should the next expressly requested full RC493 candidate be built. Report total and per-chapter page counts, sparse continuations, keep exceptions, largest voids by page type, runtime/memory, and changes from the same-source baseline. Review all pages at contact-sheet scale and every exceptional keep/continuation and scenario opening in detail. **A lower page total is accepted only with preserved content and equal or better readability.** No arbitrary percentage target is supported by this pass, and physical proof remains **NOT TESTED** until actual printed sheets are examined.

For repeatable QA, retain a compact evidence bundle for each candidate: exact source/style/font/asset identities and PDF hashes; all-page geometry CSV with method limitations; independently enumerated source-unit and serialized-anchor comparisons; stable-ID keep/continuation findings; a baseline/trial settings manifest with measured outputs; labelled renders/contact sheets and actual review scope; navigation and cream/white coordinate comparisons; and separate physical-proof status. Reproduction should begin by verifying the PDF hash, then independently extracting the recorded C.5/T327 pages, comparing actual anchor pages with the map, and inspecting the named rendered pages. Research renders and private references remain ignored working evidence, not release assets. This pass's method notes are the [pipeline audit](../research_notes/Rulebook%20layout%20and%20density%20review/pipeline_audit.md) and [reference/design research](../research_notes/Rulebook%20layout%20and%20density%20review/reference_design.md).

The repository baseline passed **203 tests with 3 expected skips** during this review. That verifies the existing development baseline, not these proposed changes. Official Typst recommendations must be checked against the locked installed version before adopting any new API. The next useful deliverable is a trustworthy, measured specimen comparison and a proposed final profile for owner review; it is not another unmeasured global shrink or a new publication approval.
