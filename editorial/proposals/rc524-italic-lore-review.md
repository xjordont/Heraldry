## Verdict

The wider lore is in very good shape and agrees with Section 1 as revised. Every Boss, faction, legend, and artifact cross-reference checks out: the Forsaken entries name Captain Veyr, the Giant Black Widow and Deep Elf Priest point to the Broodmother, the Pale City entries defer to the White Court and the Magistrate's unadjourned court, the Mire entries share the silence-of-frogs motif with Vossegar, the Khorrun and Sun-Crowned Prince entries match the Sun-Drowned lore, and the artifact lore for Cael, Vane, Kest, Salttide, Wode, Karnessa, the Hollow Crown, and the Voice repeats Section 1's facts exactly. > Want made them wiry, stubborn, and hard to finish.

## Checked and left alone

- **'The Mire' versus 'the mire'.** The bestiary uses the capitalized proper name for the climate and the lowercase common noun for the swamp itself ('the mire's deepest channels'). The split is consistent enough to read as intentional.
- **'the burning years', 'the long winter', 'the long famine'.** Descriptive phrases, not the capitalized Fall-names of 1.3.1; only 'the long silence' in the Satyr entry collides with a named one.
- **'a man's height', 'twice a man's height', 'hurl grown men aside'.** Measures and idioms; the book uses them throughout and they do not name a person.
- **Tomb Guardian's 'a thousand years'.** The priest-king tombs predate the Fall by far; no conflict with the two-century Fall.
- **Generic Black Dragon versus Vossegar, Green Dragon versus Maelthorn.** The Elite entries describe lesser kin in the same climates; the Bosses are the named apex. No conflict.
