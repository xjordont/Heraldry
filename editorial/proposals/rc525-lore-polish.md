# Final Section 1 polish (RC525)

**Applied as RC525 on 2026-10-31** under the owner's instruction "Is the lore more perfect now? If not make it so." A fresh read of Section 1 after RC523 and RC524 found five sentence-level flaws, two of them introduced by the RC523 edits. Each is an exact replacement archived in `editorial/history/RC525.json`. No rule, number, table cell, or closed list changed, and no new world fact was introduced beyond a Bastion type already described under Saltspire.

### POLISH-01

1.3.2 geography: 'the sea' twice in one sentence after RC523's salt-coast repair.

**Before**

> The great forest spreads west to the sea, the ice locks the north,

**After**

> The great forest spreads west to the coast, the ice locks the north,

### POLISH-02

1.3.3 Bastion types: the list named four kinds of holding for six Bastions and omitted Saltspire's tower-city.

**Before**

> a walled town, a fortress-chapel, a sealed mining city, a stilt-town behind its toll-chains.

**After**

> a walled town, a fortress-chapel, a sealed mining city, a tower-city around a well, a stilt-town behind its toll-chains.

### POLISH-03

Sablekeep: 'registration of every caster' sat against 1.3.4, where the Watch hangs sorcerers and druids rather than registering them.

**Before**

> the registration of every caster beneath its banner.

**After**

> the registration of every caster it suffers to live beneath its banner.

### POLISH-04

1.3.5 old peoples: 'gnoll raiders' duplicated the goblinoid paragraph and gnolls are raiders, not an old people or border power.

**Before**

> dryad ambassadors, deep elves, nagas, gnoll raiders, necromancers, lizardfolk temple-guards,

**After**

> dryad ambassadors, deep elves, nagas, necromancers, lizardfolk temple-guards,

### POLISH-05

1.3.6 patrons: 'chasing a debt' and 'debt-holders calling in what they are owed' said the same thing twice in one list.

**Before**

> Merchant houses chasing a debt, chapels hungry for relics,

**After**

> Merchant houses chasing a lost cargo, chapels hungry for relics,

## Verdict after RC525

Section 1 now reads clean end to end. Every climate names the power that rules it and every Boss is foreshadowed where a reader will meet it. The geography, the Fall's timeline, the caster laws, and the proverbs agree with the rest of the book. The remaining choices I deliberately left alone are the deliberate paradox "inherited punishment without inheritance" in 1.3.1, the summary box's echo of the body text in 1.3, and the "a man's height" measures throughout the bestiary, all of which read as intended.
