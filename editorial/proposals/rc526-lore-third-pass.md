## Verdict

Nothing structural remains. ## Findings

### LORE3-01: 1.3 glance box

**Recommended.** The glance box repeats, nearly verbatim, the body paragraph eight lines below it ('belongs to the dead ... the old powers that waited out the Fall. Adventurers are the few who ...'). A summary should compress, not duplicate; the lyrical version stays in the body.

**Current text**

> Six **Bastions** hold what remains of mortal civilization; everything between them belongs to the dead and to the old powers that waited out the Fall. Adventurers are the few who go out into that country anyway.

**Proposed text**

> Six **Bastions** hold what remains of mortal civilization. Everything between them is contested ground, and adventurers are the few who are paid to cross it.

### LORE3-02: 1.3.2 Ashlands entry

**Recommended.** The 1.3.2 introduction already says the dead empire 'sprawl[s] across the eastern heart' and that the roads run 'from the Ashlands to every climate'. The entry repeated both phrases on the same page; the wheel image is kept.

**Current text**

> They sprawl across the eastern heart of the world, where the imperial roads still run out to every climate like spokes from a shattered wheel.

**Proposed text**

> They are the hub of the world, and the imperial roads still run out from them like spokes from a shattered wheel.

### LORE3-03: 1.3.5 Khorrun passage

**Recommended.** 'Handwriting that matches no living scribe' is Ysolde Vane's signature (1.3.7 and both of her 7.17.3 items). Khorrun borrowing it dilutes hers; the replacement is the dracolich's own.

**Current text**

> each recording exactly who has taken what, in handwriting no living scribe would claim. Parties do not hunt the five.

**Proposed text**

> each recording exactly who has taken what, in a hand that has not been warm since the Fall. Parties do not hunt the five.

## Checked and deliberately left alone

- **Chapter 11's reprise of 1.3.6.** The campaign opener repeats Section 1's six-climate litany, the 'Small enough to hide' proverb, and the contract list in varied words. It is a refrain for readers who arrive at Chapter 11 after a dozen adventures, and the variation is enough. Changing it would weaken the campaign chapter's opening.
- **Paragraph endings.** Twenty-six of Section 1's eighty-six lore paragraphs end on a negation ('It heals nothing', 'It does not buy innocence'). That cadence is the book's voice and no two adjacent paragraphs share it. Left as is.
- **'Still' (86 uses), 'patient', 'count', 'toll', 'ledger'.** Thematic vocabulary for a world that keeps accounts. Frequencies are high but earned.
- **'The dead empire' versus 'the old empires'.** Both appear. The book reads as a succession of empires, the last of which is 'the dead empire' of the Ashlands; the usage is consistent with that.
- **'A voice that does not belong to them'.** Used twice (Deepwarden's contract clause and Kest's Reliquary objective). Two uses of a possession motif is a pattern, not a repeat.
- **Spelling and Oxford comma.** Clean. The sweep's 'labor' hits are 'laboratory'; its numerals are all in italic stat lines and worked examples.
