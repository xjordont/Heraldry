Reviewer: Codex delegated scenario-map reviewer. Review date: 2026-10-15 (America/Winnipeg); final evidence recorded 2026-10-16 UTC. Scope: FIG-21 through FIG-56, all 36 Core Scenario maps.

Source: `source/Heraldry_RC493.md`, SHA-256 `c8e72ea694fde5c85df112514b5aaaabd03b34900c143eab082a2f73b5c039dd`. The live contracts and canon supplement were read. Integration, 2026-10-16: current dependency bindings now also include the already reviewed 2.6 measurement, 2.9.1 terrain, and shared 10.1 rules for every map. All 36 original maps were rendered and viewed on nine contact sheets. All 36 final maps were then individually viewed at their intended 243 pt column width, rendered directly from SVG to PNG at 144 ppi (486 pixels wide), with the bundled fonts. Changed maps were also inspected during iteration. The reviewer compared each map and caption with the matching active scenario's setup, objective, Boss, random-group, deployment and extraction text, plus shared 10.1 placement/default rules and applicable terrain classifications. Printed counts are representative where the key or map footer explicitly says so. Hidden contents and delayed combatants were checked for accidental disclosure or premature placement.

## Applied repairs

- FIG-24: reduced the central label/ruler congestion and separated two overlapping Resting Tarantula glyphs within their existing legal Webbed Section.
- FIG-27: moved the platform dimension line outside the Dome and moved the Lens label/leader away from the hazards.
- FIG-29: moved the Unstable label off the Group 1 ring.
- FIG-32: explained that door markers are the hinges/lock Binding Points.
- FIG-36: removed a redundant floor outline that visually closed the otherwise open Command Tent entrance.
- FIG-47: made the intentionally undrawn, variable Pressure Plate setup explicit.
- FIG-51: removed a potentially misleading G2 position in outer Shallow Water; retained its required state and patrol instruction as a location-qualified note. No murky-water boundary was invented.
- FIG-53 and FIG-55: cleared surrounding water hatching from raised land/island features. FIG-55 now labels Ritual Island explicitly. Integration follow-up also corrected FIG-53's uniform-water depiction: its base is Difficult deep mud, with three illustrative Shallow Water pools and white raised Hummocks. This uses the existing terrain glyphs and preserves positions/counts of objectives and combatants.

Opt-in `knockout` and `label_at` renderer fields support the narrow visual fixes. Existing coordinates, printed mechanical distances, states, counts and triggers were preserved, except the two illustrative Tarantula positions moved farther apart within the same existing Webbed Section. `scenario_specs.json` now identifies its RC493 source. Shared FIG-20/57 changes were coordinated with the integration owner; their final review belongs to that owner, not this report.
