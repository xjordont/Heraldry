## Resolved false alarms

- **3 TAP** does appear in current main rules, but all six examined occurrences are explicit Journey penalties from a 4-TAP default. They are not conflicting statements of the ordinary allowance. Replacing every 3 TAP with 4 TAP would erase existing consequences.
- **Druidic Rituals** is not a recent heading-only drift: class grants, the casting rules, and the reference summary agree, and the wording predates the publication repository.
- The rule that a reference summary cannot override the full rule already exists in Appendix C. The supplied brief's issue is its location label; there is no need to add a second precedence mechanic or renumber the manuscript.
- Existing automatic armour mitigation does not consume the defender's Reaction. Treating the absence of Brace as an omitted entry would alter established combat timing.
- A Monster's ordinary damage or Morale roll is not an ability Check. Preserving the no-Checks rule does not remove its separately specified dice procedures.

## Recommended owner decision

1. Confirm retaining **4 TAP**, **Druidic Rituals**, and **Appendix C. Rules Reference** in future-work instructions, with all existing exceptions and full-rule precedence.
2. Confirm retaining **automatic Chest AD / AV under 5.4.3**, **Block for shield mitigation**, and **no Monster Checks under 2.3**, including the higher-LVL casting restriction. Align the brief to those existing procedures rather than adding Brace or an ML Check.
