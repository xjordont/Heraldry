LINE: a confident, slightly heavier outer contour around each main form; thinner interior lines; line weight thins with distance. TONE: built only from ink marks - parallel hatching that follows the form, crosshatching for deep shade, sparse stippling for grit, ash and dust - plus a few deliberate solid-black shapes (eye sockets, open mouths, doorways, cast shadows, the darkest folds).
