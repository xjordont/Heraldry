SOLDIER - CLASSIC FANTASY PEN-AND-INK ILLUSTRATION

Create a gritty, old-school black-and-white ink illustration of a Soldier background: discipline in war; starting kit Spear, in the dark fantasy world of Heraldry Skirmish, drawn the way a human illustrator inked the interior art of a late-1970s or 1980s fantasy rulebook or adventure module: brush, dip pen and India ink on paper, with the bite of a woodcut.

FORMAT AND ISOLATION
Square 1:1 composition. Pure black ink on warm ivory-white paper, with no colour, lettering, borders, or decorative framing.
A still-life arrangement isolated on blank ivory-white paper, resting on a small surface that fades out in a few sparse strokes. No figure and no background scenery.
Leave at least 8% clean white margin on every side; nothing touches or is cropped by the image edge.

COMPOSITION
Still life, a tall spear leaning against a battered kit chest, a folded surcoat and a dented cap of iron hanging on the spear shaft.
A small still life of the tools, garments and keepsakes of this background's trade; no figure, so it never resembles a class or ancestry portrait. Isolated on white paper; no background scenery.

SUBJECT DETAILS
* Spear with a leaf-shaped head and a worn ash shaft
* Dented open kettle helm hung on the shaft
* Folded plain surcoat with no heraldry
* Iron-bound kit chest with a worn lock
* Hobnailed boots set neatly beside
* Whetstone and a rag on the chest lid
* Worn belt with an empty scabbard loop

SOURCE TEXT
Depict this faithfully and invent nothing that contradicts it:
"A soldier marched where they were told and fought when they were ordered, and learned that discipline keeps people alive longer than courage alone. War has settled into them now, and sits easy on the shoulders."

ARTISTIC STYLE AND ATMOSPHERE
Weary order, war settled easily on the shoulders.
Ink it like gritty old-school rulebook monster and character art: a bold, rough brush contour of uneven weight, heavy solid-black shadow shapes, and scratchy hatching or dry-brush drag where form turns from light into shadow. High contrast, with few in-between textures; small human imperfections such as broken lines and dry-brush streaks are welcome. It reads as a strong silhouette first and detail second: no stippled skin, no uniform micro-texture, no over-rendered surfaces. Light falls from the upper left. Believable anatomy, armour construction, weapon grips and materials, worn, scarred and weathered. It must look drawn by hand with brush, pen and ink, not generated or rendered.

FINAL VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
Do not draw:
* No figure or hands, and no lettering.
* No banners with symbols.
* Not the Fighter class's kit.
Avoid photorealism, digital-render polish, clean vector-like line, stippled or noisy texture, painterly shading, smooth gradients, grey wash or grey ink tones, airbrush, colour, typography, readable runes, watermarks, signatures, modern objects, cartoon or anime proportions and cropped limbs. The finished artwork should be gritty, bold and readable at small size and print crisply in pure black ink, like original ink art in an old-school fantasy rulebook.

STYLE REFERENCES
The attached reference images are gritty, old-school ink art by human illustrators. Match their bold brush contours, heavy solid blacks, scratchy hatching, dry-brush texture, contrast and finish, but do not copy their subjects or compositions, and draw in pure black ink only.
