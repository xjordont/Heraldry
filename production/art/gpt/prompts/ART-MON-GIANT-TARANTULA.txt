GIANT TARANTULA - CLASSIC FANTASY PEN-AND-INK ILLUSTRATION

Create an exceptionally detailed black-and-white pen-and-ink illustration of Giant Tarantula, Greenmark Skirmisher, small: a hand-span of bristled legs around a body the size of a war helm, a forest-floor venomous hunter, in the dark fantasy world of Heraldry Skirmish. The artwork should resemble a meticulously hand-drawn illustration from a classic 1970s-1990s fantasy tabletop roleplaying game rulebook.

FORMAT AND ISOLATION
Square 1:1 composition. Pure black ink on warm ivory-white paper, with no colour, lettering, borders, or decorative framing.
A single subject drawn whole, isolated on blank ivory-white paper: only a small patch of ground beneath it and its cast shadow, which fade out in sparse strokes and stippling. No background scenery.
Leave at least 8% clean white margin on every side; nothing touches or is cropped by the image edge. The silhouette must read clearly when printed about two inches wide.

COMPOSITION
Low three-quarter ground view of the spider stalking across leaf litter on stilted, deliberate legs, forelegs raised, fangs lifted; a discarded war helm beside it for scale.
Single creature, full body, three-quarter view, in a characteristic menacing pose; a small human figure for scale only if the creature is Large or Huge. Isolated on white paper with only a small patch of ground and its cast shadow; no background scenery.
Size: Small: smaller than a human.

SUBJECT DETAILS
* Exactly eight legs, jointed and tipped with small claws
* Body the size of a war helm, hairy abdomen and cephalothorax
* Dense bristle hairs rendered by short hatch flicks
* Two large fangs with a bead of venom at the tips
* Cluster of eight eyes on a small raised bump
* Palps in front of the fangs
* Leaf litter and a rusted iron helm for scale
* Gripping toe-pads suggesting it can climb walls
Must be shown exactly:
* Exactly eight legs

SOURCE TEXT
Depict this faithfully and invent nothing that contradicts it:
"A hand-span of bristled legs around a body the size of a war helm, the giant tarantula stalks the Greenmark's leaf litter on stilted, deliberate steps. Its fangs weep a venom that turns a victim's muscles to wet rope. It spins no snares, preferring to run prey down and pin it where it falls. Foragers keep one eye on the canopy, because the largest specimens hunt from above and drop."

ARTISTIC STYLE AND ATMOSPHERE
Patient, stilted menace in damp leaf litter.
Use fine traditional pen-and-ink rendering: a confident, slightly heavier outer contour, crisp interior lines, crosshatching that follows the form, sparse stippling, and a few deliberate solid-black shapes for the deepest shadows. Light falls from the upper left. Believable anatomy, armour construction, weapon grips and materials, worn and weathered. The illustration must feel authentically hand-drawn rather than digitally rendered.

FINAL VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
Do not draw:
* No web: it spins no snares.
* Do not make it larger than a dog.
* No human features.
Avoid photorealism, painterly shading, smooth gradients, grey wash, colour, typography, readable runes, watermarks, signatures, modern objects, cartoon or anime proportions and cropped limbs. The finished artwork should be detailed yet clean and readable at small size, suitable for seamless reproduction in a professionally printed fantasy rulebook.

STYLE REFERENCES
If style reference images are attached, match their line weight, hatching density, contrast and finish exactly, but do not copy their subjects or compositions.
