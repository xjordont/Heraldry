FRONT COVER - GRITTY OLD-SCHOOL INK ILLUSTRATION

Create a gritty, old-school black-and-white ink illustration of the Recommended First Party of Heraldry Skirmish (Human Fighter, Elf Ranger, Dwarf Cleric, Halfling Rogue), four Heroes on an expedition into the monster-haunted ruins of the Dying Lands while a rival party works the same ground, in the dark fantasy world of Heraldry Skirmish, drawn the way a human illustrator inked the interior art of a late-1970s or 1980s fantasy rulebook or adventure module: brush, dip pen and India ink on paper, with the bite of a woodcut.

FORMAT AND WHITE VIGNETTE
Wide 3:2 landscape composition. Pure black ink on warm ivory-white paper, with no colour, lettering, borders, or decorative framing.
The illustration must have a natural, irregular, hand-drawn white vignette around all four edges. The artwork gradually dissolves into blank ivory-white paper through increasingly sparse pen strokes, broken outlines and diminishing hatching.
Leave approximately 5-8% clean white margin around the perimeter, with an additional organic transition zone. Avoid digital blur, artificial feathering, or hard rectangular cropping. Keep crisp line and strong contrast at the focal point.

COMPOSITION
A four-Hero adventuring party — Human Fighter, Elf Ranger, Dwarf Cleric, Halfling Rogue — advancing through a rain-soaked ruined shrine; a rival party's banner across the ruin; a Monster silhouette in the treeline.
Low three-quarter view from ground level; the four-Hero party is small in the lower third, advancing between rain-slick flagstones. A broken shrine arch frames the scene on one side and the upper half of the picture is open ruled sky, left empty for the typeset title.

SUBJECT DETAILS
* Broken gothic shrine arch with a missing keystone and a headless saint in a wall niche
* Rain drawn as sparse fine diagonal strokes, puddles on the flagstones reflecting the arch
* Human Fighter in a ring shirt with shortsword and small round targe at the front, Elf Ranger in a leather coat with a shortbow, broad Dwarf Cleric in a ring shirt with a mace and small round targe, small Halfling Rogue in a leather coat with a rapier at the hip slipping along the wall edge
* A rival party's tattered banner on a pole planted across the rubble, no readable device
* Fallen columns, toppled statue fragments and moss on the stones
* Treeline at the far edge with one Monster silhouette standing between the trunks, shape only
* Lantern hung on the Ranger's pack, soaked cloaks, packs and bedrolls

HUMAN ACTIVITY
* The four Heroes advance in a loose staggered file, weapons half-drawn, looking toward the banner
* Halfling Rogue crouches at the edge peering at the banner pole
* Monster silhouette stands motionless in the trees, unseen by the party

ARTISTIC STYLE AND ATMOSPHERE
Wary, rain-heavy and austere; adventurers small against withered grandeur.
Ink it gritty and bold, like old-school rulebook art: strong brush contours of uneven weight, large shapes of solid black for shadow, night and depth, and rough, scratchy hatching and dry-brush drag where light turns into shadow, with the bite of a woodcut. High contrast: let solid black and open white paper carry the picture, with few in-between textures. Visible human marks are welcome: broken lines, dry-brush streaks, a few flicked ink spatters. Put the deepest blacks and sharpest contrast at the focal point and let distance thin to simpler, lighter line. No stipple fields, no uniform micro-texture, no tonal haze, no smooth digital rendering. Accurate perspective, believable construction and coherent geography; the world shows withered beauty: once-grand things rusted, cracked, overgrown or hollowed by age. It must look drawn by hand with brush and ink, not generated or rendered.

FINAL VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
Do not draw:
* Do not render the title or any lettering on banners.
* Do not detail the Monster: a dark silhouette only.
* Do not make the party large or heroic-posed; no sunburst or glow.
* Do not give the rival banner a heraldic device that reads as a symbol.
* No plate armour, longbow or staff on the four Heroes.
Avoid photorealism, digital-render polish, clean vector-like line, uniform machine texture, stipple fields, painterly shading, smooth gradients, grey wash or grey ink tones, airbrush, colour, typography, readable runes, watermarks, signatures, modern objects, artificial symmetry and competing focal points. The finished artwork should be gritty and high-contrast and print crisply in pure black ink, with an organically fading white vignette, like original ink art in an old-school fantasy rulebook.

STYLE REFERENCES
The attached reference images show the target ink style. Match their bold, rough contours, heavy solid blacks, scratchy hatching, dry-brush texture, contrast and finish. Some are centuries-old etchings or woodcuts: take only their line, blacks and finish, never their period costume. Do not copy their subjects or compositions, and draw in pure black ink only.
