GNAWTHOUGHT - GRITTY OLD-SCHOOL INK ILLUSTRATION

Create a gritty, old-school black-and-white ink illustration of Gnawthought, a Ratkin Psion with an Alchemist background: a small-sized, child-sized, twitchy, chemical-stained mind-fighter, named for the gnawed hem and the chewed-off glove tips, in the dark fantasy world of Heraldry Skirmish, drawn the way a human illustrator inked the interior art of a late-1970s or 1980s fantasy rulebook or adventure module: brush, dip pen and India ink on paper, with the bite of a woodcut.

FORMAT AND ISOLATION
Tall 2:3 portrait composition. Pure black ink on warm ivory-white paper, with no colour, lettering, borders, or decorative framing.
A single subject drawn whole, isolated on blank ivory-white paper: only a small patch of ground beneath it and its cast shadow, which fade out in a few sparse strokes. No background scenery.
Leave at least 8% clean white margin on every side; nothing touches or is cropped by the image edge. The silhouette must read clearly when printed about two inches wide.

COMPOSITION
Full figure in three-quarter view in a wary half-crouch, dagger held low in one hand, the other hand raised with a stoppered acid pot ready to throw.
Single full-length Hero standing alone, drawn whole from the top of the head to the soles of the feet, in a ready three-quarter pose that shows the listed ancestry, class, background and every listed item of equipment. Borderless and isolated on blank white paper with no background of any kind: only a few sparse strokes of ground and a short cast shadow directly under the feet.

ARTISTIC STYLE AND ATMOSPHERE
Skittish, bright and dangerous.
Ink it like gritty old-school rulebook monster and character art: a bold, rough brush contour of uneven weight, heavy solid-black shadow shapes, and scratchy hatching or dry-brush drag where form turns from light into shadow. High contrast, with few in-between textures; small human imperfections such as broken lines and dry-brush streaks are welcome. It reads as a strong silhouette first and detail second: no stippled skin, no uniform micro-texture, no over-rendered surfaces. Light falls from the upper left. Believable anatomy, armour construction, weapon grips and materials, worn, scarred and weathered. It must look drawn by hand with brush, pen and ink, not generated or rendered.

FINAL VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
Do not draw:
* Do not make it a plague-ridden scavenger or a monster Ratkin Warrior or Scavenger.
* Do not draw it as a literal rat: upright, humanoid, clothed.
* No glowing eyes or aura.
* No staff or books.
* No background, scenery, wall, doorway, sky or second figure: the hero stands alone on bare white paper.
* No frame, border, panel box or vignette edge.
* No lettering, runes, name or caption anywhere, including on shields, scrolls and banners.
* Nothing cropped: the whole figure and every weapon are visible from the top of the head to the soles of the feet.
* No glow, aura, magical effect or speed lines.
Avoid photorealism, digital-render polish, clean vector-like line, stippled or noisy texture, painterly shading, smooth gradients, grey wash or grey ink tones, airbrush, colour, typography, readable runes, watermarks, signatures, modern objects, cartoon or anime proportions and cropped limbs. The finished artwork should be gritty, bold and readable at small size and print crisply in pure black ink, like original ink art in an old-school fantasy rulebook.

STYLE REFERENCES
The attached reference images show the target ink style. Match their bold, rough contours, heavy solid blacks, scratchy hatching, dry-brush texture, contrast and finish. Some are centuries-old etchings or woodcuts: take only their line, blacks and finish, never their period costume. Do not copy their subjects or compositions, and draw in pure black ink only.
