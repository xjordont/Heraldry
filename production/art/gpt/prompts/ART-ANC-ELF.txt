ELF - CLASSIC FANTASY PEN-AND-INK ILLUSTRATION

Create a hand-inked black-and-white illustration of an Elf adventurer, medium-sized, tall and lean, a graceful, long-memoried traveller in a Bastion's service, in the dark fantasy world of Heraldry Skirmish, drawn the way a skilled human illustrator inked the interior art of a classic 1970s-1990s fantasy tabletop roleplaying game rulebook: dip pen, brush and India ink on paper.

FORMAT AND ISOLATION
Tall 2:3 portrait composition. Pure black ink on warm ivory-white paper, with no colour, lettering, borders, or decorative framing.
A single subject drawn whole, isolated on blank ivory-white paper: only a small patch of ground beneath it and its cast shadow, which fade out in a few sparse strokes. No background scenery.
Leave at least 8% clean white margin on every side; nothing touches or is cropped by the image edge. The silhouette must read clearly when printed about two inches wide.

COMPOSITION
Full figure in three-quarter view standing at ease on a patch of leaf litter, longbow lowered in one hand, head tilted as if listening, one hand resting on the quiver.
Single full-length adventurer of this ancestry in travel-worn gear, three-quarter view. Isolated on white paper with only a small patch of ground and its cast shadow; no background scenery.

SUBJECT DETAILS
* Tall, lean build with long limbs and a long neck
* Long pointed ears swept back, high cheekbones, calm eyes that notice much
* Long weathered travelling cloak, faded and mended, pinned at one shoulder
* Longbow taller than the Halfling's whole body, quiver of fletched arrows
* Soft boots and bracers; light, unfussy kit
* Fine, sparse hair loose or in a narrow braid
* Sparse leaf litter at its feet

SOURCE TEXT
Depict this faithfully and invent nothing that contradicts it:
"Elves are shaped by older forests and longer memories, and carry a grace that seems almost inhuman to the hurried peoples of the age. They move lightly, notice much, and bear the weight of beauty and loss without letting it show."

ARTISTIC STYLE AND ATMOSPHERE
Elegiac grace, beauty and loss held back behind composure.
Ink it like classic rulebook monster and character art: a bold, confident outer contour, clean interior lines, hatching only where form turns into shadow, and a few solid-black spot shapes for the deepest darks. Large areas stay open white paper. Simple, readable shapes over surface texture: no stippled skin, no noise, no over-rendered detail. Light falls from the upper left. Believable anatomy, armour construction, weapon grips and materials, worn and weathered. It must look drawn by hand with pen, brush and ink, not generated or rendered.

FINAL VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
Do not draw:
* Not a Deep Elf: pale skin and dark armour belong to that entry, here keep light open clothes.
* Do not pose it drawing the bow: it is at ease.
* No glowing or magical effects.
* No heavy armour.
Avoid photorealism, digital-render polish, hyper-detail, stippled or noisy texture, painterly shading, smooth gradients, grey wash, colour, typography, readable runes, watermarks, signatures, modern objects, cartoon or anime proportions and cropped limbs. The finished artwork should be clean, bold and readable at small size, like original ink art in a professionally printed fantasy rulebook.

STYLE REFERENCES
The attached reference images are hand-inked art by human illustrators. Match their line quality, economy of line, hatching density, amount of open white paper and finish, but do not copy their subjects or compositions.
