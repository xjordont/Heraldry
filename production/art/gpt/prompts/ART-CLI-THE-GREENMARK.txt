THE GREENMARK - CLASSIC FANTASY PEN-AND-INK ILLUSTRATION

Create an exceptionally detailed black-and-white pen-and-ink illustration of the Greenmark, the great temperate forest of the surviving world, larger than the maps, where roots take roads and the forest does not recognize human law, in the dark fantasy world of Heraldry Skirmish. The artwork should resemble a meticulously hand-drawn illustration from a classic 1970s-1990s fantasy tabletop roleplaying game rulebook.

FORMAT AND WHITE VIGNETTE
Wide 3:2 landscape composition. Pure black ink on warm ivory-white paper, with no colour, lettering, borders, or decorative framing.
The illustration must have a natural, irregular, hand-drawn white vignette around all four edges. The artwork gradually dissolves into blank ivory-white paper through increasingly sparse pen strokes, broken outlines, diminishing crosshatching, and scattered stippling.
Leave approximately 5-8% clean white margin around the perimeter, with an additional organic transition zone. Avoid digital blur, artificial feathering, or hard rectangular cropping. Preserve crisp detail and strong contrast throughout the central composition.

COMPOSITION
A forest road at ground level in deep woods, roots lifting the paving, trees seeming to lean in; a moss-warm grove circle opens in the middle distance.

SUBJECT DETAILS
* Ancient oaks with fluted trunks and heavy canopy
* Road slabs broken and swallowed by roots, a milestone half-buried and tilted
* Foresters' warning signs cut in bark as slashes and notches, not letters
* Upright hound-like shapes between trunks, dark and indistinct
* Grove-circle of standing mossy stones with low mist and a patch of bare paper where snow has melted
* Fern, bracken, fallen logs, toadstools
* Light shafts as blank paper between trunks

SOURCE TEXT
Depict this faithfully and invent nothing that contradicts it:
"The Greenmark is the great temperate forest of the surviving world. It is larger than the maps. Roots take a road within a season and make the mile-stones useless. Travellers report trees shifting at the edge of sight, upright hounds between trunks, and grove-circles where moss stays warm after snow. Foresters cut warning signs into bark for those who know how to read them. The most common sign means turn back even if the voice ahead is your own. The forest is no wilderness. It is a country that does not recognize human law."

ARTISTIC STYLE AND ATMOSPHERE
Ancient, watchful and indifferent to humans.
Use exceptionally fine traditional pen-and-ink rendering, with varied line weights, intricate crosshatching, stippling, textured bark and timber grain, irregular stonework and carefully constructed architecture. Strong foreground contrast and progressively lighter distant detail create atmospheric depth. Maintain accurate perspective, believable construction and coherent geography. The world shows withered beauty: once-grand things rusted, cracked, overgrown or hollowed by age. The illustration must feel authentically hand-drawn rather than digitally rendered.

FINAL VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
Do not draw:
* No people; the climate is the subject.
* Do not make the grove circle the focus: it is a small mossy ring of stones in the middle distance, so this is not the Whispering Grove scenario (standing stones in a clearing as the whole subject).
* Do not detail the hound shapes: barely seen silhouettes only.
* No letters in the bark signs.
Avoid photorealism, painterly shading, smooth gradients, grey wash, colour, typography, readable runes, watermarks, signatures, modern objects, artificial symmetry and competing focal points. The finished artwork should be exceptionally detailed yet visually readable, with an organically fading white vignette suitable for seamless reproduction in a professionally printed fantasy rulebook.

STYLE REFERENCES
If style reference images are attached, match their line weight, hatching density, contrast and finish exactly, but do not copy their subjects or compositions.
