DWARF - CLASSIC FANTASY PEN-AND-INK ILLUSTRATION

Create an exceptionally detailed black-and-white pen-and-ink illustration of a Dwarf adventurer, medium-sized but broad and heavy, from sealed stone halls and old craft traditions, shown as a hardened traveller in a Bastion's service, in the dark fantasy world of Heraldry Skirmish. The artwork should resemble a meticulously hand-drawn illustration from a classic 1970s-1990s fantasy tabletop roleplaying game rulebook.

FORMAT AND ISOLATION
Tall 2:3 portrait composition. Pure black ink on warm ivory-white paper, with no colour, lettering, borders, or decorative framing.
A single subject drawn whole, isolated on blank ivory-white paper: only a small patch of ground beneath it and its cast shadow, which fade out in sparse strokes and stippling. No background scenery.
Leave at least 8% clean white margin on every side; nothing touches or is cropped by the image edge. The silhouette must read clearly when printed about two inches wide.

COMPOSITION
Full figure in three-quarter view, braced wide-stanced, lantern held forward in one hand with a round shield strapped on that forearm, handaxe ready in the other hand.
Single full-length adventurer of this ancestry in travel-worn gear, three-quarter view. Isolated on white paper with only a small patch of ground and its cast shadow; no background scenery.

SUBJECT DETAILS
* Very broad shoulders, short thick limbs, stocky frame, shorter than the Human but heavier
* Long braided beard with metal rings, heavy brow, deep-set eyes used to the dark
* Heavy mail or plated chest armour worn with ease, scuffed and re-riveted
* Round shield strapped on the lantern arm, handaxe in the other hand
* Lantern with a shuttered iron housing
* Thick hobnailed boots planted on a small patch of worn flagstone
* Stone-cut craftsmanship in belt buckle and axe head
Must be shown exactly:
* Clearly shorter than an Elf or Orc, but broad and heavy

SOURCE TEXT
Depict this faithfully and invent nothing that contradicts it:
"Dwarves are cut from sealed stone halls, old grudges, and craft traditions older than most fallen kingdoms. Hardship did not sour them so much as harden them, and little in this world meets one of them head-on and walks away whole."

ARTISTIC STYLE AND ATMOSPHERE
Granite stubbornness, a person built to be struck and remain standing.
Use fine traditional pen-and-ink rendering: a confident, slightly heavier outer contour, crisp interior lines, crosshatching that follows the form, sparse stippling, and a few deliberate solid-black shapes for the deepest shadows. Light falls from the upper left. Believable anatomy, armour construction, weapon grips and materials, worn and weathered. The illustration must feel authentically hand-drawn rather than digitally rendered.

FINAL VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
Do not draw:
* Not a comic dwarf: serious proportions, no oversized hat or cartoon nose.
* No horned helmet.
* Do not give it a greataxe (that is the Orc adventurer's kit).
* No stair, doorway or other scenery.
Avoid photorealism, painterly shading, smooth gradients, grey wash, colour, typography, readable runes, watermarks, signatures, modern objects, cartoon or anime proportions and cropped limbs. The finished artwork should be detailed yet clean and readable at small size, suitable for seamless reproduction in a professionally printed fantasy rulebook.

STYLE REFERENCES
If style reference images are attached, match their line weight, hatching density, contrast and finish exactly, but do not copy their subjects or compositions.
