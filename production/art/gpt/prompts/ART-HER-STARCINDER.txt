STARCINDER - GRITTY OLD-SCHOOL INK ILLUSTRATION

Create a gritty, old-school black-and-white ink illustration of Starcinder, an Elf Wizard with an Artisan background: a medium-sized, slender craftsperson-mage, named for the cut-star inlays and singed sleeves, in the dark fantasy world of Heraldry Skirmish, drawn the way a human illustrator inked the interior art of a late-1970s or 1980s fantasy rulebook or adventure module: brush, dip pen and India ink on paper, with the bite of a woodcut.

FORMAT AND ISOLATION
Tall 2:3 portrait composition. Pure black ink on warm ivory-white paper, with no colour, lettering, borders, or decorative framing.
A single subject drawn whole, isolated on blank ivory-white paper: only a small patch of ground beneath it and its cast shadow, which fade out in a few sparse strokes. No background scenery.
Leave at least 8% clean white margin on every side; nothing touches or is cropped by the image edge. The silhouette must read clearly when printed about two inches wide.

COMPOSITION
Full figure in three-quarter view, upright and balanced, a fine masterwork quarterstaff held two-handed with the lower end braced. Tonal key: pale robe, dark apron. Silhouette: a very tall slim vertical with a staff.
Single full-length Hero standing alone, drawn whole from the top of the head to the soles of the feet, in a ready three-quarter pose that shows the listed ancestry, class, background and every listed item of equipment. Borderless and isolated on blank white paper with no background of any kind: only a few sparse strokes of ground and a short cast shadow directly under the feet.

FINAL VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
Do not draw:
* Not the Sorcerer: no wild windblown hair.
* No pointy star-covered hat.
* No spell effects, fire or ice drawn.
* Not a court elf: no ornate finery.
* No tattered, torn, ragged, frayed or fringed cloth anywhere: every hem and cloak edge is cleanly cut or neatly hemmed, with at most one or two small neat patches.
* No uniform all-over hatch mesh or stipple fields: use expressive local scratchy hatching and crosshatching on shadowed forms, balanced with solid blacks and open white highlights.
* No tint, tone or colour of any kind, including on foliage, skin and metal: pure black ink on white paper only.
* No background, scenery, wall, doorway, sky or second figure: the hero stands alone on bare white paper.
* No frame, border, panel box or vignette edge.
* No lettering, runes, name or caption anywhere, including on shields, scrolls and banners.
* Nothing cropped: the whole figure and every weapon are visible from the top of the head to the soles of the feet.
* Nothing touches the edge: keep at least 10% clean white margin on all four sides, and no stray specks, spatter or ground strokes outside the figure and its short shadow.
* No glow, aura, magical effect or speed lines.
Avoid photorealism, digital-render polish, clean vector-like line, stippled or noisy texture, all-over mechanical crosshatching, painterly shading, smooth gradients, grey wash, grey or coloured tint, airbrush, typography, readable runes, watermarks, signatures, modern objects, cartoon or anime proportions, tattered or ragged cloth, stray specks, cropped limbs and anything touching the image edge. The finished artwork is pure black ink on white, bold and readable at small size, with at least 10% clean white margin on every side, and prints crisply, like original ink art in an old-school fantasy rulebook.
