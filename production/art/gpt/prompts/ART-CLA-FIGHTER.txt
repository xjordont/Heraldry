FIGHTER - CLASSIC FANTASY PEN-AND-INK ILLUSTRATION

Create an exceptionally detailed black-and-white pen-and-ink illustration of a Fighter, Martial class: drilled, line-holding soldier of fortune; packages of Shortsword, Targe, Ring Shirt, Dagger, in the dark fantasy world of Heraldry Skirmish. The artwork should resemble a meticulously hand-drawn illustration from a classic 1970s-1990s fantasy tabletop roleplaying game rulebook.

FORMAT AND ISOLATION
Tall 2:3 portrait composition. Pure black ink on warm ivory-white paper, with no colour, lettering, borders, or decorative framing.
A single subject drawn whole, isolated on blank ivory-white paper: only a small patch of ground beneath it and its cast shadow, which fade out in sparse strokes and stippling. No background scenery.
Leave at least 8% clean white margin on every side; nothing touches or is cropped by the image edge. The silhouette must read clearly when printed about two inches wide.

COMPOSITION
Full figure in three-quarter view braced low with a small round targe held forward, shortsword drawn back over the hip, taking an unseen charge.
Single full-length adventurer in an action-ready pose with class-typical arms and gear, three-quarter view. Isolated on white paper with only a small patch of ground and its cast shadow; no background scenery.

SUBJECT DETAILS
* Ring shirt (mail of linked rings) over a padded arming coat
* Targe: small round shield with a central boss, edge dented
* Plain shortsword, single-handed, held ready
* Dagger on belt
* Open-faced helm or coif, no visor
* Stance planted wide, weight forward, knuckles white
* Scuffs and rust on mail, boots braced on a small patch of cracked flagstone

SOURCE TEXT
Depict this faithfully and invent nothing that contradicts it:
"A Fighter is forged in places where hesitation gets people killed. Drill, repetition, and hard lessons carve in the instinct to hold ground when holding it is the only thing that matters. They have watched lines collapse, and they know to the heartbeat how long one can stand before it does."

ARTISTIC STYLE AND ATMOSPHERE
Stoic resolve, the last thing between the line and collapse.
Use fine traditional pen-and-ink rendering: a confident, slightly heavier outer contour, crisp interior lines, crosshatching that follows the form, sparse stippling, and a few deliberate solid-black shapes for the deepest shadows. Light falls from the upper left. Believable anatomy, armour construction, weapon grips and materials, worn and weathered. The illustration must feel authentically hand-drawn rather than digitally rendered.

FINAL VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
Do not draw:
* Not heavy plate: ring shirt only.
* Do not give it a two-handed weapon (that is the Berserker or Ravager package).
* No flourish or duel pose.
* No gate, wall or other scenery.
Avoid photorealism, painterly shading, smooth gradients, grey wash, colour, typography, readable runes, watermarks, signatures, modern objects, cartoon or anime proportions and cropped limbs. The finished artwork should be detailed yet clean and readable at small size, suitable for seamless reproduction in a professionally printed fantasy rulebook.

STYLE REFERENCES
If style reference images are attached, match their line weight, hatching density, contrast and finish exactly, but do not copy their subjects or compositions.
