PAUPER - CLASSIC FANTASY PEN-AND-INK ILLUSTRATION

Create a hand-inked black-and-white illustration of a Pauper background: hunger and want; starting kit Club, in the dark fantasy world of Heraldry Skirmish, drawn the way a skilled human illustrator inked the interior art of a classic 1970s-1990s fantasy tabletop roleplaying game rulebook: dip pen, brush and India ink on paper.

FORMAT AND ISOLATION
Square 1:1 composition. Pure black ink on warm ivory-white paper, with no colour, lettering, borders, or decorative framing.
A still-life arrangement isolated on blank ivory-white paper, resting on a small surface that fades out in a few sparse strokes. No figure and no background scenery.
Leave at least 8% clean white margin on every side; nothing touches or is cropped by the image edge.

COMPOSITION
Still life on bare ground, a crude knobbed club leaning on a cracked clay bowl beside a heel of bread and a ragged blanket.
A small still life of the tools, garments and keepsakes of this background's trade; no figure, so it never resembles a class or ancestry portrait. Isolated on white paper; no background scenery.

SUBJECT DETAILS
* Crude wooden club with a knobbed head
* Cracked clay bowl with a crust
* Ragged patched blanket
* Single worn shoe
* Rusty tin cup
* A few cobblestones under the bowl
* Rope-belted sack

SOURCE TEXT
Depict this faithfully and invent nothing that contradicts it:
"A pauper came up from the bottom, where every crust and every coin had to be fought for. Want made them wiry, stubborn, and stubbornly hard to finish."

ARTISTIC STYLE AND ATMOSPHERE
Bare want and stubborn survival.
Ink it like classic rulebook monster and character art: a bold, confident outer contour, clean interior lines, hatching only where form turns into shadow, and a few solid-black spot shapes for the deepest darks. Large areas stay open white paper. Simple, readable shapes over surface texture: no stippled skin, no noise, no over-rendered detail. Light falls from the upper left. Believable anatomy, armour construction, weapon grips and materials, worn and weathered. It must look drawn by hand with pen, brush and ink, not generated or rendered.

FINAL VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
Do not draw:
* No figure or hands, and no lettering.
* No pity beggar poses.
* Do not add coins.
Avoid photorealism, digital-render polish, hyper-detail, stippled or noisy texture, painterly shading, smooth gradients, grey wash, colour, typography, readable runes, watermarks, signatures, modern objects, cartoon or anime proportions and cropped limbs. The finished artwork should be clean, bold and readable at small size, like original ink art in a professionally printed fantasy rulebook.

STYLE REFERENCES
The attached reference images are hand-inked art by human illustrators. Match their line quality, economy of line, hatching density, amount of open white paper and finish, but do not copy their subjects or compositions.
