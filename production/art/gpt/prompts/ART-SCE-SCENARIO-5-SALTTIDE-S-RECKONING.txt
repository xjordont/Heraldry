SCENARIO 5: SALTTIDE'S RECKONING - CLASSIC FANTASY PEN-AND-INK ILLUSTRATION

Create a gritty, old-school black-and-white ink illustration of Salttide's Reckoning, a Mire scenario of a sacred island cursed like oil on water, where Edrava Salttide has made a ritual of the place and turns it on the living who entered without paying the toll, in the dark fantasy world of Heraldry Skirmish, drawn the way a human illustrator inked the interior art of a late-1970s or 1980s fantasy rulebook or adventure module: brush, dip pen and India ink on paper, with the bite of a woodcut.

FORMAT AND WHITE VIGNETTE
Wide 3:2 landscape composition. Pure black ink on warm ivory-white paper, with no colour, lettering, borders, or decorative framing.
The illustration must have a natural, irregular, hand-drawn white vignette around all four edges. The artwork gradually dissolves into blank ivory-white paper through increasingly sparse pen strokes, broken outlines and diminishing hatching.
Leave approximately 5-8% clean white margin around the perimeter, with an additional organic transition zone. Avoid digital blur, artificial feathering, or hard rectangular cropping. Keep crisp line and strong contrast at the focal point.

COMPOSITION
View across still oily water from the reed line toward a ritual island; reeds on both banks lean toward the island, three carved effigies stand on it with a robed figure among them.

SUBJECT DETAILS
* Reeds all bent toward the island as if drawn by wind
* Concentric oily rings spreading over the water
* Three carved driftwood effigies with crude faces
* Robed toll-lord figure on the island with raised arms, ship-prow headdress
* Crocodile snouts just breaking the surface
* Muddy islets and dense reeds
* White salt crust on the shore and a dead tree

HUMAN ACTIVITY
* The robed figure performs the rite
* Two tiny watchers crouch in the reeds

SOURCE TEXT
Depict this faithfully and invent nothing that contradicts it:
"A curse lies over the sacred island like oil on water. Edrava Salttide has made a ritual of the place, the same ritual they once used to curse the harbours that refused their ships, now turned on the living who entered The Mire without paying the toll. The reeds bend toward the island. The water smells of brine, this far from any sea. Reedmarch sent one party to break the working, and let a rival sell the other a contract whose small print it has not read."

ARTISTIC STYLE AND ATMOSPHERE
Brine-bitter curse in still air.
Ink it gritty and bold, like old-school rulebook art: strong brush contours of uneven weight, large shapes of solid black for shadow, night and depth, and rough, scratchy hatching and dry-brush drag where light turns into shadow, with the bite of a woodcut. High contrast: let solid black and open white paper carry the picture, with few in-between textures. Visible human marks are welcome: broken lines, dry-brush streaks, a few flicked ink spatters. Put the deepest blacks and sharpest contrast at the focal point and let distance thin to simpler, lighter line. No stipple fields, no uniform micro-texture, no tonal haze, no smooth digital rendering. Accurate perspective, believable construction and coherent geography; the world shows withered beauty: once-grand things rusted, cracked, overgrown or hollowed by age. It must look drawn by hand with brush and ink, not generated or rendered.

FINAL VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
Do not draw:
* Not the barrow entrance of Scenario 1.
* Do not portray Salttide: small only.
* No readable markings.
* No glow.
Avoid photorealism, digital-render polish, clean vector-like line, uniform machine texture, stipple fields, painterly shading, smooth gradients, grey wash or grey ink tones, airbrush, colour, typography, readable runes, watermarks, signatures, modern objects, artificial symmetry and competing focal points. The finished artwork should be gritty and high-contrast and print crisply in pure black ink, with an organically fading white vignette, like original ink art in an old-school fantasy rulebook.

STYLE REFERENCES
The attached reference images are gritty, old-school ink art by human illustrators. Match their bold brush contours, heavy solid blacks, scratchy hatching, dry-brush texture, contrast and finish, but do not copy their subjects or compositions, and draw in pure black ink only.
