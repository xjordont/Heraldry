EVIL EYE - CLASSIC FANTASY PEN-AND-INK ILLUSTRATION

Create a gritty, old-school black-and-white ink illustration of Evil Eye, Undermarches Elite, large: a floating orb of leathery flesh with one great central eye and a crown of writhing eyestalks, with an antimagic aura; it flies, in the dark fantasy world of Heraldry Skirmish, drawn the way a human illustrator inked the interior art of a late-1970s or 1980s fantasy rulebook or adventure module: brush, dip pen and India ink on paper, with the bite of a woodcut.

FORMAT AND ISOLATION
Square 1:1 composition. Pure black ink on warm ivory-white paper, with no colour, lettering, borders, or decorative framing.
A single subject drawn whole, isolated on blank ivory-white paper: only a small patch of ground beneath it and its cast shadow, which fade out in a few sparse strokes. No background scenery.
Leave at least 8% clean white margin on every side; nothing touches or is cropped by the image edge. The silhouette must read clearly when printed about two inches wide.

COMPOSITION
Frontal three-quarter view of the orb drifting above a small patch of cave floor, central eye wide open, four eyestalks writhing; a small human figure below, drawn small, light and secondary, for scale.
Single creature, full body, three-quarter view, in a characteristic menacing pose; a small human figure for scale only if the creature is Large or Huge. Isolated on white paper with only a small patch of ground and its cast shadow; no background scenery.
Size: Large: roughly twice a human's height or bulk.

SUBJECT DETAILS
* Round floating orb of leathery, wrinkled flesh
* One great central eye with a solid black pupil
* Crown of four writhing eyestalks, each ending in a small eye
* Fine radiating hatching around the body to suggest the antimagic aura
* Dust motes drifting around it
* No wings, no limbs
Must be shown exactly:
* Exactly one central eye and four eyestalks

SOURCE TEXT
Depict this faithfully and invent nothing that contradicts it:
"A floating orb of leathery flesh ruled by one great central eye and crowned with writhing eyestalks, the evil eye spits a different doom from each lesser eye. It drifts the deep wrapped in an aura that unmakes magic outright. No two of its glares do the same thing twice. It regards every other living thing as a competitor to be erased."

ARTISTIC STYLE AND ATMOSPHERE
Cold, hostile regard: an alien intelligence that sees every other living thing as a rival to erase.
Ink it like gritty old-school rulebook monster and character art: a bold, rough brush contour of uneven weight, heavy solid-black shadow shapes, and scratchy hatching or dry-brush drag where form turns from light into shadow. High contrast, with few in-between textures; small human imperfections such as broken lines and dry-brush streaks are welcome. It reads as a strong silhouette first and detail second: no stippled skin, no uniform micro-texture, no over-rendered surfaces. Light falls from the upper left. Believable anatomy, armour construction, weapon grips and materials, worn, scarred and weathered. It must look drawn by hand with brush, pen and ink, not generated or rendered.

FINAL VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
Do not draw:
* No large toothy mouth: not a Beholder lookalike.
* No extra eyes beyond the central eye and the four eyestalks.
* No glow rays.
Avoid photorealism, digital-render polish, clean vector-like line, stippled or noisy texture, painterly shading, smooth gradients, grey wash or grey ink tones, airbrush, colour, typography, readable runes, watermarks, signatures, modern objects, cartoon or anime proportions and cropped limbs. The finished artwork should be gritty, bold and readable at small size and print crisply in pure black ink, like original ink art in an old-school fantasy rulebook.

STYLE REFERENCES
The attached reference images are gritty, old-school ink art by human illustrators. Match their bold brush contours, heavy solid blacks, scratchy hatching, dry-brush texture, contrast and finish, but do not copy their subjects or compositions, and draw in pure black ink only.
