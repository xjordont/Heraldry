1.3.2 THE SIX CLIMATES OF RUIN - CLASSIC FANTASY PEN-AND-INK ILLUSTRATION

Create a gritty, old-school black-and-white ink illustration of the shape of the Dying Lands: broken mountains down the spine, the dead empire in the east, the great forest in the west, ice in the north, dunes in the south and swamps in the south-east, in the dark fantasy world of Heraldry Skirmish, drawn the way a human illustrator inked the interior art of a late-1970s or 1980s fantasy rulebook or adventure module: brush, dip pen and India ink on paper, with the bite of a woodcut.

FORMAT AND WHITE VIGNETTE
Wide 3:2 landscape composition. Pure black ink on warm ivory-white paper, with no colour, lettering, borders, or decorative framing.
The illustration must have a natural, irregular, hand-drawn white vignette around all four edges. The artwork gradually dissolves into blank ivory-white paper through increasingly sparse pen strokes, broken outlines and diminishing hatching.
Leave approximately 5-8% clean white margin around the perimeter, with an additional organic transition zone. Avoid digital blur, artificial feathering, or hard rectangular cropping. Keep crisp line and strong contrast at the focal point.

COMPOSITION
A sweeping panorama from the spine of the broken mountains with all six climates under one sky: forest to the west, dead city to the east, ice to the north, dunes to the south and drowned fen to the south-east, with dark cavern mouths in the mountainside below; a lone surveyor with a staff stands in the foreground.

SUBJECT DETAILS
* Jagged mountain crest with a cairn and a rock ledge foreground
* Western forest as dense hatched mass running to a pale coast
* Eastern city skyline of snapped towers and domes in fine lines
* Northern ice fields with glacial walls and thin hatching
* Southern dunes with tomb-teeth along the horizon
* South-eastern fen of reeds, black pools and drowned trees in sparse fine lines
* Dark cavern mouths and old mine adits in the mountain flank below the ledge (the Undermarches)
* Imperial roads as straight lines radiating from the east across the lands
* Surveyor with a staff, a rolled map and a leather satchel

HUMAN ACTIVITY
* The surveyor stands at the cairn looking across the lands, one hand raised to shade eyes

SOURCE TEXT
Depict this faithfully and invent nothing that contradicts it:
"Beyond the walls, land has become allegiance. Expedition ledgers still say forest, city, cavern, ice, desert, and swamp. Survivors know better. They use older names. The shape of the world is plain enough to anyone who has walked it. The broken mountains run down its spine. The dead empire and its ruined cities sprawl across the eastern heart. The great forest spreads west to the salt-coast, the ice locks the north, the dunes burn in the south beyond the mountain passes, and the swamps drown the south-east where the rivers give out at the sea. The old imperial roads still join all of it, straight and patient, running out from the Ashlands to every climate that survived. A dry label cannot warn a traveller that a road has memory, a court still sits without lungs, or a pool of black water may be listening."

ARTISTIC STYLE AND ATMOSPHERE
Immense, quiet and unsettled.
Ink it gritty and bold, like old-school rulebook art: strong brush contours of uneven weight, large shapes of solid black for shadow, night and depth, and rough, scratchy hatching and dry-brush drag where light turns into shadow, with the bite of a woodcut. High contrast: let solid black and open white paper carry the picture, with few in-between textures. Visible human marks are welcome: broken lines, dry-brush streaks, a few flicked ink spatters. Put the deepest blacks and sharpest contrast at the focal point and let distance thin to simpler, lighter line. No stipple fields, no uniform micro-texture, no tonal haze, no smooth digital rendering. Accurate perspective, believable construction and coherent geography; the world shows withered beauty: once-grand things rusted, cracked, overgrown or hollowed by age. It must look drawn by hand with brush and ink, not generated or rendered.

FINAL VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
Do not draw:
* Not a mountain-pass vista with a party and a single road toward one city.
* No dragons or creatures.
* No map lettering.
* No hard compass-rose graphic.
Avoid photorealism, digital-render polish, clean vector-like line, uniform machine texture, stipple fields, painterly shading, smooth gradients, grey wash or grey ink tones, airbrush, colour, typography, readable runes, watermarks, signatures, modern objects, artificial symmetry and competing focal points. The finished artwork should be gritty and high-contrast and print crisply in pure black ink, with an organically fading white vignette, like original ink art in an old-school fantasy rulebook.

STYLE REFERENCES
The attached reference images are gritty, old-school ink art by human illustrators. Match their bold brush contours, heavy solid blacks, scratchy hatching, dry-brush texture, contrast and finish, but do not copy their subjects or compositions, and draw in pure black ink only.
