GREYTHORN - CLASSIC FANTASY PEN-AND-INK ILLUSTRATION
Create an exceptionally detailed, panoramic black-and-white pen-and-ink illustration of Greythorn, a fortified medieval forest town in the dark fantasy world of Heraldry Skirmish. The artwork should resemble a meticulously hand-drawn illustration from a classic 1970s–1990s fantasy tabletop roleplaying game rulebook.
FORMAT AND WHITE VIGNETTE
Wide 3:2 landscape composition. Pure black ink on warm ivory-white paper, with no colour, lettering, borders, or decorative framing.
The illustration must have a natural, irregular, hand-drawn white vignette around all four edges. The artwork gradually dissolves into blank ivory-white paper through increasingly sparse pen strokes, broken outlines, diminishing crosshatching, and scattered stippling.
Leave approximately 5–8% clean white margin around the perimeter, with an additional organic transition zone. Avoid digital blur, artificial feathering, or hard rectangular cropping. Preserve crisp detail and strong contrast throughout the central composition.
COMPOSITION AND TOWN SCALE
View Greythorn from an elevated forested hillside, looking down across an ancient woodland road toward the fortified settlement.
Greythorn is a substantial medieval town of approximately 10,000 inhabitants, occupying a broad forest clearing roughly 1–2 km across. Its scale should resemble a significant medieval market town rather than a village, isolated fort, or castle.
The town occupies approximately 70–80% of the central composition's width. Hundreds of visible rooftops extend through successive layers of streets and buildings toward distant palisades, suggesting thousands of structures beyond those individually distinguishable.
The settlement follows gently rising terrain, with dense residential districts, commercial streets, workshops, and open civic spaces. Its distant buildings become progressively smaller and lighter, establishing convincing depth and urban scale.
Two enormous, ancient oak trees frame the foreground, their gnarled trunks, exposed roots, and spreading branches forming a natural canopy without obscuring the town.
A winding forest road leads across a weathered wooden bridge spanning a rocky stream toward the fortified main gate.
GREYTHORN'S FORTIFICATIONS
Greythorn's defensive perimeter consists primarily of tall sharpened timber palisades, reinforced earthworks, elevated wooden fighting platforms, and regularly spaced watchtowers.
A substantial timber gatehouse stands in the middle foreground, with two square defensive towers flanking an open entrance. Guards watch arriving travellers, while rangers patrol the battlements.
The fortifications are functional, weathered, and repeatedly repaired, reflecting a practical frontier settlement rather than an imperial capital.
THE TOWN INTERIOR
Beyond the walls, a dense and architecturally coherent medieval townscape spreads across the clearing.
Buildings consist predominantly of half-timbered houses, workshops, and warehouses with steep shingled or thatched roofs, exposed beams, narrow windows, wooden balconies, and irregular smoking chimneys.
Winding streets, courtyards, small squares, and crowded residential blocks create an organic medieval settlement.
Include several recognizable landmarks:

* A tall central wooden watchtower with a weathered banner.
* A substantial timber council hall.
* An exceptionally large military training yard with archery targets and practising rangers.
* A modest chapel and a small, understated arcane spire.
* A busy crossroads market with merchant stalls and wagons.
* The Hollow Quarter, a secluded lower district incorporating ancient trees, wooden walkways, and small bridges.

Integrate these landmarks naturally into the urban fabric without allowing individual buildings to dominate the overall townscape.
ANCIENT FOREST AND STANDING STONES
Dense primeval woodland surrounds Greythorn. Towering oaks, twisted branches, tangled roots, ferns, moss-covered boulders, and thick undergrowth create the impression of a settlement surviving within an ancient, powerful forest.
In the left foreground, beside the road, place several weathered standing stones carved with spirals and indistinct runes. Small ritual offerings of bundled branches, herbs, and woven baskets rest at their bases, marking the site of Greythorn's annual tithe to the forest.
Subtle supernatural details may reward close inspection, such as an indistinct woodland figure partially concealed among distant trees. Avoid prominent magical effects.
HUMAN ACTIVITY
Populate the roads, bridge, gatehouse, market, and training yard with numerous realistically scaled figures engaged in ordinary activity.
Include cloaked rangers carrying longbows, guards with spears, foresters carrying axes, merchants, burdened horses, supply wagons, apprentices, and townspeople.
Their activities should convey a prosperous but vigilant frontier settlement, without distracting from the architecture and landscape.
ARTISTIC STYLE AND ATMOSPHERE
The atmosphere is grounded, austere, mysterious, and subtly ominous. Greythorn is a resilient bastion of civilization maintained through vigilance and uneasy agreements with the ancient forest.
Use exceptionally fine traditional pen-and-ink rendering, with varied line weights, intricate crosshatching, stippling, textured bark, weathered timber grain, irregular stonework, and carefully constructed rooftops.
Strong foreground contrast and progressively lighter distant detail create atmospheric depth. Maintain accurate perspective, believable construction, and coherent urban geography.
The illustration must feel authentically hand-drawn rather than digitally rendered.
FINAL VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
Prioritize the expansive fortified town and its densely inhabited architecture, framed by monumental ancient oaks. The winding road, wooden bridge, gatehouse, and standing stones establish the foreground narrative.
Avoid elaborate stone castles, exaggerated fantasy towers, artificial symmetry, competing focal points, photorealism, painterly shading, smooth gradients, colour, typography, watermarks, and modern objects.
The finished artwork should be exceptionally detailed yet visually readable, with a convincing medieval urban scale, immersive forest atmosphere, and an organically fading white vignette suitable for seamless reproduction in a professionally printed fantasy rulebook.
