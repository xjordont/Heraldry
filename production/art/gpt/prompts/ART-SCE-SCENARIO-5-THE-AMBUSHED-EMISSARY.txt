SCENARIO 5: THE AMBUSHED EMISSARY - CLASSIC FANTASY PEN-AND-INK ILLUSTRATION

Create a hand-inked black-and-white illustration of the Ambushed Emissary, a Greenmark scenario where an emissary carrying documents sealed under two rival Bastion laws vanished on the western forest road and is now held in a makeshift bandit camp deep in the wood, in the dark fantasy world of Heraldry Skirmish, drawn the way a skilled human illustrator inked the interior art of a classic 1970s-1990s fantasy tabletop roleplaying game rulebook: dip pen, brush and India ink on paper.

FORMAT AND WHITE VIGNETTE
Wide 3:2 landscape composition. Pure black ink on warm ivory-white paper, with no colour, lettering, borders, or decorative framing.
The illustration must have a natural, irregular, hand-drawn white vignette around all four edges. The artwork gradually dissolves into blank ivory-white paper through increasingly sparse pen strokes, broken outlines and diminishing hatching.
Leave approximately 5-8% clean white margin around the perimeter, with an additional organic transition zone. Avoid digital blur, artificial feathering, or hard rectangular cropping. Keep crisp line and strong contrast at the focal point.

COMPOSITION
Dusk view from behind a screen of roots and ferns looking across a small clearing at a makeshift bandit camp; the bound emissary sits by a post near a lean-to while a reeve stands guard. Dropped clues are strewn across the lower foreground.

SUBJECT DETAILS
* Makeshift camp of hide lean-tos, a smoking fire pit and a rough palisade of stakes (cover only, not a fort)
* Emissary in fine but mud-stained robes, hands bound to a post, a satchel strap across their chest
* Forest-road reeve in half-armour with a longsword at the hip and a hand crossbow
* Clues in the foreground: a broken cart wheel, a snapped sealed document tube with no readable writing, torn cloth caught on a thorn, a cut rope hanging from a branch
* Tall oaks with small clearings and patches of undergrowth
* Dusk sky ruled lightly, crows returning to roost
* Boot tracks and wagon ruts leading off toward the road
Must be shown exactly:
* The emissary looks like a courier or diplomat

HUMAN ACTIVITY
* Two bandits sit at the fire, one sharpening a blade
* The reeve stands over the emissary, arms folded
* Two tiny watchers crouch in the ferns at the bottom edge

SOURCE TEXT
Depict this faithfully and invent nothing that contradicts it:
"An emissary vanished on the western forest road carrying documents sealed under two rival Bastion laws. One patron in Greythorn wants the courier found breathing. Another, further off, wants the satchel and does not ask after the courier. The deep wood has kept worse secrets, and the Hollow Quarter is not telling."

ARTISTIC STYLE AND ATMOSPHERE
Tense, furtive espionage in the dark wood.
Draw it with confident hand-inked line: clean outlines of varied weight made with dip pen and brush, carrying the slight natural irregularity of a human hand. Build the picture from clearly drawn shapes, not surface texture. Use hatching and crosshatching selectively, only to model form and shadow, and keep generous areas of open white paper; let distance thin to simple, lighter outline. Put detail where the eye should land and suggest the rest with a few telling strokes. No stipple fields, no noise-like texture, no tonal haze, no over-rendered surfaces. Accurate perspective, believable construction and coherent geography; the world shows withered beauty: once-grand things rusted, cracked, overgrown or hollowed by age. It must look drawn by hand, not generated or rendered.

FINAL VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
Do not draw:
* Not the Bandit's Toll barricade or the road ambush itself.
* No readable seals or documents.
* No violence against the emissary.
* Not an adventurer: the emissary is a courier or diplomat.
Avoid photorealism, digital-render polish, hyper-detail, uniform texture everywhere, painterly shading, smooth gradients, grey wash, colour, typography, readable runes, watermarks, signatures, modern objects, artificial symmetry and competing focal points. The finished artwork should read cleanly and print crisply, with an organically fading white vignette, like original ink art in a professionally printed fantasy rulebook.

STYLE REFERENCES
The attached reference images are hand-inked art by human illustrators. Match their line quality, economy of line, hatching density, amount of open white paper and finish, but do not copy their subjects or compositions.
