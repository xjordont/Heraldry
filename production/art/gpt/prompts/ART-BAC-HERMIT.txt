HERMIT - GRITTY OLD-SCHOOL INK ILLUSTRATION

Create a gritty, old-school black-and-white ink illustration of a Hermit background: solitary silence; starting kit Quarterstaff, in the dark fantasy world of Heraldry Skirmish, drawn the way a human illustrator inked the interior art of a late-1970s or 1980s fantasy rulebook or adventure module: brush, dip pen and India ink on paper, with the bite of a woodcut.

FORMAT AND ISOLATION
Square 1:1 composition. Pure black ink on warm ivory-white paper, with no colour, lettering, borders, or decorative framing.
A still-life arrangement isolated on blank ivory-white paper, resting on a small surface that fades out in a few sparse strokes. No figure and no background scenery.
Leave at least 8% clean white margin on every side; nothing touches or is cropped by the image edge.

COMPOSITION
Still life on a flat stone, a gnarled staff lying beside a tin cup and a bowl, a single stool and a cold fire ring.
A small still life of the tools, garments and keepsakes of this background's trade; no figure, so it never resembles a class or ancestry portrait. Isolated on white paper; no background scenery. PHYSICAL PLAUSIBILITY (owner review 2026-10-13): every object rests on a surface, leans against a visible support, or hangs from a visible hook, strap, peg or nail, and it obeys gravity: nothing floats or juts out unsupported, cloth and rope hang downward, heavy things sit low, a balance scale tips toward its heavier pan. Each object is the true medieval European form of the thing named, drawn correctly: no modern items (fountain pens, wristwatches, zippers) and no East Asian weapons (no kunai, shuriken or ninja gear).

SUBJECT DETAILS
* Gnarled staff with a knotted top
* Tin cup and wooden bowl, empty

SOURCE TEXT
Depict this faithfully and invent nothing that contradicts it:
"A hermit withdrew from the world, or was left behind by it. Silence, hunger, and long solitude taught them to listen for older truths and to depend on no one."

ARTISTIC STYLE AND ATMOSPHERE
Utter solitude, bare and silent.
Ink it like old-school rulebook equipment art: a bold, rough contour of uneven weight around each object, heavy solid-black shadow shapes, and scratchy hatching or dry-brush drag that follows each material: grain on wood, dents and pits on metal, folds and wear on leather and cloth. High contrast, with few in-between textures. Each object reads as a clear silhouette first and detail second: no stipple fields, no uniform micro-texture, no over-rendered surfaces. Light falls from the upper left. Believable construction, worn and used. It must look drawn by hand with brush, pen and ink, not generated or rendered.

FINAL VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
Do not draw:
* No figure or hands, and no lettering.
* No companion animals.
* Do not add a hut interior full of clutter.
* Nothing floats or juts out unsupported; every object rests, leans or hangs from a visible support.
Avoid photorealism, digital-render polish, clean vector-like line, stippled or noisy texture, painterly shading, smooth gradients, grey wash or grey ink tones, airbrush, colour, typography, readable runes, watermarks, signatures, modern objects, cartoon or anime proportions and cropped limbs. The finished artwork should be gritty, bold and readable at small size and print crisply in pure black ink, like original ink art in an old-school fantasy rulebook.
